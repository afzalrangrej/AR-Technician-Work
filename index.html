All Equipment & Appliances Covered

Shop & Home Repair Services

We bring specialized tools to your doorstep or handle deep component repairs in our Kosamba workshop bench.

Domestic & Industrial Gas Stoves

Repair & servicing for LPG gas stoves, commercial bhattis, industrial gas burners, pipeline fittings, regulators, and leak prevention.

Electric Infrared & Induction Cooktops

Component-level repairs for infrared cookers and induction stoves, heating coils, IGBT circuit boards, sensor errors, and touch panels.

Mixers & Industrial Mixture Machines

Motor rewinding, jar coupling replacement, blade sharpness, and heavy-duty repairs for domestic mixers & commercial industrial grinders.

Pressure Cookers & Kitchen Appliances

Complete servicing for domestic & commercial pressure cookers, whistle weights, rubber gaskets, safety valves, handles, and body reshaping.

Agricultural Spray Pumps (Dawa Ke Pump)

Battery-operated farm sprayers, pesticide knapsack pumps, 12V pressure motor repair, battery replacement, charger port, and nozzle cleaning.

Coolers, Fans & Complete Electrical Wiring

Air cooler motor & water pump repair, ceiling/exhaust fan winding, capacitor fix, and complete home & commercial electrical wiring ("Kuchh bhi wiring").
